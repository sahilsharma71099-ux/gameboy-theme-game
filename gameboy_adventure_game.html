TRAINER GUIDE

Welcome to the Emerald RPG Engine! Explore routes, talk to locals, and battle wild monster in tall grass.

W, A, S, D / Arrows: Walk
Z / Space / Enter: Interact / Confirm

M / ESC: Pause Menu
H: Open Help Guide
Touch D-Pad: On-Screen Controls

🏥 Visit the monster Center to heal your team anytime.
